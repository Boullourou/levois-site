# La Ligne claire — prompt de la planche conceptuelle

Mode : outil intégré imagegen. Concept d'interface, aucun actif produit extrait de la planche.

Create an exceptional senior-design-agency concept board for a French real-estate decision experience named LEVOIS. One single art direction: LA LIGNE CLAIRE, an annotated editorial reading. Show FOUR complete mobile WEBSITE screenshots, each 390px wide, side by side in the upper portion, no phones, no browser/device mockups; then one generous DESKTOP WEBSITE composition beneath. High resolution, exact legible French typography. Board has thin dark separators, ample margins, a small external title 'La Ligne claire'. The actual product uses very light paper #f7f5ef, ink #202c33, restrained vermilion #ae412c. Georgia serif for prominent headlines and annotations, Public Sans for body and controls. Low density, precise editorial rules, open numbered fragments, marginal notes, underlined phrases, no cards, no boxes, no dashboard, no gradients, no stock real estate photography, no fake metrics, no invented testimonials, no hero badges. Main text is code-native UI intended for HTML/CSS.

Text 'Partir de votre situation, comprendre vos options, décider de la suite.' Then three strong open numbered navigation rows 'Acheter', 'Vendre', 'Vendre et acheter', each with fine rule and slender arrow. Separate modest link 'Explorer les repères locaux'. An editorial margin note, not an invented claim. Warm calm rigor, large white space.
