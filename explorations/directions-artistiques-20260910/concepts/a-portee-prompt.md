## Prompt initial

Outil : imagegen intégré. Deux références locales inspectées : quartier illustré existant et portrait réel de Mouaad. Planche de quatre états mobiles et d’une composition desktop. Les références sont employées comme actifs du concept ; aucune nouvelle preuve immobilière n’est produite.

Use case: product-mockup. Create a high-resolution artistic UI concept board for the French real estate decision service LEVOIS, direction “À portée”. This is a complete interface study, not a marketing poster: four large readable mobile web screen compositions side by side (390×844 proportions) and one generous desktop composition below. Flat screenshots, NO phone/device mockups, no perspective, no faux screen borders. Label each artboard discreetly outside the product: Accueil, Visites, Recherche, Lecture, Desktop.
Art direction: agency-quality humane precise editorial design; limestone background #f3f0e8, deep pine text #264b42, terracotta accents #a44029. Headings Bricolage Grotesque (expressive rounded grotesque with generous counters) and body Satoshi. Existing serif LEVOIS wordmark small at top. Airy but immediately useful, refined large type, fine horizontal rules, choice ribbons with clipped or gently folded ends, a terracotta edge and a paper sheet opening to reveal the consequence. No card grids, no dashboard, no blue, no gradient, no generic icon rows, no hero badges. Spacious touch-friendly controls. Text is real French UI. Header has LEVOIS at left and small return arrow/menu at right. Small separate exploration strip can appear above header.
Image 1 is the EXISTING illustrated miniature neighborhood: use as warm lived-in imagination, never as geographic evidence. Place in an ample horizontal crop on Accueil after introductory copy, small caption “Quartier illustré · une évocation, pas une carte”. Do not reproduce its pale empty sky across all views. Image 2 is the REAL portrait of Mouaad: preserve his identity exactly and use ONLY in the bottom human handoff section of Lecture and desktop continuation, not elsewhere. Do not invent people.
SCREEN 1 Accueil: headline exactly “Un projet immobilier. Des choix plus clairs.” with expressive three-line pine text; supporting text “Partir de votre situation, comprendre vos options, décider de la suite.” Then neighborhood crop, then three full-width stacked unboxed choice ribbons “Acheter”, “Vendre”, “Vendre et acheter”, arrow at far right. Separate underlined text link “Explorer les repères locaux”. All three project choices visible in viewport.
SCREEN 2 Visites: “Des visites, mais aucune offre ?” Then immediate value in soft paper emphasis “Une visite sans offre ne suffit pas à expliquer ce qui freine la vente.” Question “Qu’est-ce qui revient après les visites ?” Three ribbons “Le même point revient”, “Les retours sont différents”, “Je n’ai pas de retour précis”. Last selected with pine fill and ivory type. Open paper consequence below, marked in terracotta: “Un retour concret à construire.” Footer text “Ces retours ne suffisent pas à conclure sur le prix ou sur la qualité du bien.” CTA “Préparer la prochaine question”. No price conclusion.
SCREEN 3 Recherche: “Qu’est-ce que votre prochain logement doit rendre possible ?” Choice ribbons “Travailler au calme”, “Recevoir mes proches”, “Profiter du dehors”; first selected. Small text “Une priorité à la fois. Les autres restent ouvertes.” Signature outlined CTA “Voir ce que cette réponse change”. Open paper below with terracotta folded corner and line “Un espace où fermer la porte.” Mini OPEN comparison table, not cards: Logement A “92 m² · pièce indépendante · séjour compact · jardin 35 m²” versus Logement B “105 m² · coin bureau dans le séjour · séjour ouvert · balcon 6 m²”. Note “Deux exemples fictifs. Aucun choix d’achat déduit.” CTA “Garder cette priorité”.
SCREEN 4 Lecture: “Votre projet, un peu plus clair.” A pale open page, left terracotta strip, section sequence: “Ce qui est compris” / “Vous cherchez un espace pour travailler au calme.”; “Ce qui paraît solide” / “Une pièce indépendante mérite d’être examinée.”; “Ce qui peut évoluer” / “La forme du logement reste ouverte.”; “Ce qui reste à vérifier” / “Budget, localisation, nuisances et disponibilité.”; “La prochaine décision utile” / “Vérifier comment vous travaillerez au quotidien.” Compact correction text link. Then “Garder cette lecture” button; lower continuation shows portrait of Mouaad and title “Un regard humain sur ce qui reste ouvert.” No forms or personal-data fields.
DESKTOP: a different, open two-column composition of Recherche, 1440×800 proportions: expressive headline on left with three horizontal ribbon choices, right generous opened paper containing comparison on equal dimensions and terracotta underline on the independent-room distinction. Very clear space, typographic rhythm, footnote and human continuation lower down. Four essential screens and desktop must be readable enough to use as implementation specification. No invented metrics, no claims about active property listings or prices, no testimonials, no further screens. Use the exact text above; reduce decorative elements before reducing type size.
